Realtek
-------
- https://www.realtek.com/

SoC vendor (RSDK, Linux 4.9.x BSP) and USB WiFi/BLE (RTL8822CU) supplier seen in the Wyze Cam Floodlight Pro (see [Wyze](wyze.md)); also used by [Gwell](gwell.md) in the Wyze Cam OG line. A camera built on a Realtek SoC is out of scope for thingino, which is Ingenic-SoC only.

### Known devices

| Device                  | SoC      | Flash              | WiFi                 | Notes                                              |
|-------------------------|----------|--------------------|----------------------|----------------------------------------------------|
| Wyze Cam Floodlight Pro | RTS3916  | FM25S01A SPI NAND  | RTL8822CU (USB)      | Secure boot, Linux 4.9.217 RSDK 6.5.0, ttyS1 57600 |
