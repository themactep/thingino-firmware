This camera has no ignition input.

Ignition & Parking

What the camera does when the car's ignition turns off. Changes apply at the next ignition change; no restart.

Parking

On: with the ignition off, continuous recording pauses and only impacts record event clips. Off: the camera keeps recording as if driving (24/7 with the power-off below set to never).

Parked sensitivity

Deviation from rest that starts an event clip while parked. Lower is more sensitive: 0.3 catches a door knock, 1 a firm bump. Driving uses the [telematics] impact_g threshold.

Power off

The camera shuts down cleanly this long after the ignition turns off, unless it comes back on first. 0 = never (watch the car battery).
